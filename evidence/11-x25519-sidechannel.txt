  CONCLUSION: outliers occur at a similar rate in EVERY class AND in the
  control operation, which has no scalar input at all. That is the
  signature of scheduler/interrupt noise, not scalar-dependent timing.
  X25519's longer runtime (9x the control) gives it proportionally
  more opportunity to be preempted, which explains its larger tail.
