7. BUGS FOUND DURING THIS AUDIT (all fixed)
   B1 CRITICAL (tooling): static-audit sections contained a LITERAL BACKSPACE
      byte (0x08) where a regex "\b" was intended, silently disabling two
      rules. Section 5 therefore reported SAFE while two unredacted peer-IP
      prints existed. Control chars stripped; audit now correctly FAILS on
      them, and the two prints were routed through netlog_safe_addr().
   B2 (test): live-test scanned all 1084 bytes for a 2-byte EIU magic.
      Measured collision rate 1.57%/link => 4.65%/run, causing a genuinely
      flaky failure. Replaced with a structural offset check. 8/8 stable.
      The 4-byte AIRB scan collides 0/20000 and was kept.
